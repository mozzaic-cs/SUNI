# Write a blog post end-to-end

Use for writing a blog post or article about a topic.

## Steps
1. **Research** - `web_search` + `web_fetch` for current facts and angles.
2. **Outline** - title options, a hook, 3-5 sections, a conclusion/CTA.
3. **Draft** - write in the requested voice; concrete examples over generalities.
4. **Tighten** - cut filler; verify every factual claim against a source.
5. Offer to save as PDF or email it.
