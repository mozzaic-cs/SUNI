# Write an SEO-optimised article

Use for writing an SEO article targeting a keyword.

## Steps
1. Confirm the primary keyword + audience. `web_search` related terms and the current
   top results to see what is expected.
2. Structure: H1 with the keyword, an intro that answers intent fast, H2/H3 sections,
   a FAQ, a conclusion. Use the keyword naturally - no stuffing.
3. Produce a meta title (<60 chars) and meta description (<160 chars).
4. Keep claims accurate and sourced; SEO never justifies fabrication.
