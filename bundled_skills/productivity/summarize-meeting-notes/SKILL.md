# Summarise meeting notes or a transcript

Use for summarising meeting notes or a transcript.

## Steps
1. Read the transcript (`read_file` or pasted text).
2. Extract: **Decisions**, **Action items** (with owner + due date if stated),
   **Open questions**, and a 3-line TL;DR.
3. Keep it faithful - do not invent owners or dates that were not said.
4. Offer to email the summary to attendees.
